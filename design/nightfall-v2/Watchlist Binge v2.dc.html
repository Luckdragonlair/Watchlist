A–Z index · accessible behavior
ROLEA single adjustable control (a slider) named "Jump to letter", whose value is the current letter. It is not 27 separate buttons, so the narrow strip doesn't count as 27 tiny targets.
TOUCH AREAThe rail is drawn 22px wide but responds to touch 44px wide along the full height. The cards make room for it, so card controls never sit under that touch area.
TAP AND DRAGTapping a letter jumps straight to it. Dragging scrubs through the letters, showing the large letter bubble and giving a light haptic tick per letter. Letters with no shows are dimmed and skipped.
TALKBACKSwipe up or down with one finger to step to the next or previous letter that has shows, using the standard increase and decrease actions. Each change is announced, for example "D, 5 shows". Double-tap and hold then drag works as a sighted drag.
KEYBOARDThe rail is one Tab stop. ↑ and ↓ step through letters, Home and End jump to # and Z, and typing a letter jumps to it. Focus stays on the rail and the list scrolls.
FALLBACKThe rail is only a shortcut. Search and the pinned section heading always do the same job, and the rail is hidden at 175% text size and above, or when the list has fewer than 30 shows.
