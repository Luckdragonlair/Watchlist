BImport

Anything that comes from your master list rather than TVmaze gets a dashed "UNVERIFIED" tag. Progress saves automatically, so a long import can be spread over several sessions. Entries are split into queues: "Needs your choice" for ambiguous or unverified entries, and "Likely matches" for single, clear results, which you can review and approve as a group. The queues are a proposal for the coding side. Matching is always a choice you make. A Watched entry with no season number needs a separate confirmation before it can apply to every episode. Import is mostly a PC task. The Android version is in section D.

TITLESWrap to at most 2 lines at normal text size, and 3 lines at 175% and above, then end with "…". A tooltip is never the only way to read the full title. Tapping or clicking the title opens the detail page, where it's always shown in full, and the accessible name always includes the full title.
CHECKBOX LABELThe episode code never breaks across lines. At large text sizes, "watched" can drop to a second line, and the row grows to fit.
LARGE TEXTUse rem units throughout. At 130% the Android card grows taller rather than cutting anything off, and the bottom tab labels stay a single word.
SMALL PHONETested at 320px wide. Arrows grow to 48px. Filter labels stay as full words: "Ready", "Upcoming" and "Not for me" all fit at 320px.
COUNTSLarge counts use separators (1,284). The status pill wraps on Android instead of cutting off text.
FFor the coding side: fonts offline

The mockups load their fonts from Google Fonts for preview only. The app itself should bundle them so it looks the same offline. All three fonts are free under the SIL Open Font License, so no paid assets or services are needed.

FONTWEIGHTS TO SHIPUSED FORFALLBACK STACK
Instrument Serif400 · regular + italicHeadings, titlesGeorgia, 'Times New Roman', serif
IBM Plex Sans400 · 500 · 600Body, buttons'Segoe UI', Roboto, system-ui, sans-serif
IBM Plex Mono500 · 600Episode codes, labelsConsolas, 'Roboto Mono', ui-monospace, monospace
  1. Self-host Latin-subset .woff2 files (about 8 files, roughly 250 KB in total) and add them to the cached app files, so they load offline from the first run.
  2. Use font-display: swap so text appears immediately, plus size-adjust on the fallback fonts so the layout doesn't jump when the real fonts load.
  3. Preload only Instrument Serif 400 and Plex Sans 400. Those two cover the page title and the first cards.
  4. Use rem units for font sizes so Android's large-text setting carries through, as in section E.
GAccessibility tests beyond 130%

At 200% text the Android card restacks. The title gets up to three lines, the checkbox row grows, and the grip and arrows move to a row of three 56px buttons along the bottom of the card. Nothing is cut off and nothing depends on hover. Browser zoom follows the width breakpoints: a 1280px window at 150% zoom gets the compact PC layout, and at 200% zoom it gets the phone layout.
