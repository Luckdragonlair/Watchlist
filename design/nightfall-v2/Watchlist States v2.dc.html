States, dialogs and the phone menu
Status messages use plain words and always pair an icon shape with the text, so color is never the only signal. Each dialog shows its first control with the keyboard focus ring. Version 2. "Saved" now fades to an icon, the completion prompt becomes a lighter "What next?" popup (a bottom sheet on Android), and only one notice shows at a time on Android, with Undo first. Conflict and restore now save automatic recovery copies before replacing anything. This version also adds the Android restore, delete and What next sheets. See also Screens v2, Binge v2 and Flows v2.

BDialogs

Every dialog that replaces or deletes data lists exactly what will change and what won't. Before a conflict choice or a restore replaces anything, the app saves an automatic recovery copy, so nothing is ever lost for good. In the conflict and restore dialogs, Export backup is a full button and never hidden behind a link. Focus stays inside the dialog, and Esc does the same as Cancel. "What next?" is meant to feel like a reward: it has a lighter backdrop and a celebratory header, and every button says where the suggested show will go. It's deferred until the core watchlist is working.

DAndroid: menu, conflict and update

On the phone, backup, account, install and sync live in the ⋯ menu at the top right of every page. Export and Restore sit together at the top of the menu. A conflict or restore opens as a full-height screen, and delete and What next open as bottom sheets. Only one notice shows at a time: Undo comes first, and the update notice waits.
